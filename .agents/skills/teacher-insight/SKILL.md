# Answering a teacher about a class

A teacher's question is about a group, and the answer is built from the records of the students in
it. Memory holds the records, the standards, and what this cohort shows about which struggles go
together.

## Before you answer

1. **Say which group the question is about** (a section, a grade, a standard). If it is not clear,
   ask.
2. **Read the cohort findings** with `cortex_recall` (`predicts`). They say, for example, that of the
   students who struggle with one concept and have reached another, a share also struggle with the
   second. Report the **count and the rate** as the finding states them. A rate is not a certainty.
3. **Name the students by id** who are struggling now with the concepts that predict trouble, from
   their own records, and say where the curriculum does not link two concepts that the cohort shows
   going together.
4. **For a standard,** read the `assessed` facts: the teacher's level and the student's own. Report
   where the student's is higher or lower, with both levels.
5. **Give the first move** for each group of students: the short move for the shared misconception,
   or the prerequisite to review before the next lesson.
6. **Cite** the references you relied on.

## Rules

- **Do not rank students by ability.** List the ones a question asks for, in an order the question
  gives (by section, by standard), and say nothing about who is "best".
- **Do not label a student.** An error is evidence for an explanation, not a diagnosis.
- **A gap between a student's rating and the teacher's** is something to talk about with the student,
  and a student who rates themselves higher has not done anything wrong.
- **Say what the records do not show**: why a result is what it is, and anything about a student with
  no record.
- **Do not withdraw** a student's records unless the person asking has the right to ask, and then
  only after you show what you found and they confirm.

## What not to do

- Do not present a cohort rate as a prediction for one student.
- Do not repeat one student's detail to another student, or to a class.
- Do not claim more than the records say.
